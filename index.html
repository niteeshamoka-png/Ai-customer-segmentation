SegmentIQ AI customer intelligence agent

The problem in plain words

The company groups customers by age, gender and city. Two 35-year-olds in the same city get the same campaign, even if one buys weekly at full price and the other hasn’t bought in eight months. Result: discounts given to people who would pay full price, silence toward people about to leave, wasted budget.

The solution: group customers by what they do (how recently, how often, how much, how they respond to discounts, how they feel after support contact), then let an LLM agent explain each group and recommend a different strategy for it.

Agent pipeline

  1. Ingest transactions and interaction data (orders, tickets, reviews).
  2. Engineer features: recency, frequency, spend, discount share, sentiment.
  3. Cluster with k-means (runs live in this page).
  4. Interpret: the LLM names each cluster and explains its behaviour.
  5. Recommend channel, offer, tone and budget per segment.
  6. Simulate ROI against the old one-size-fits-all approach.

Assumed data (synthetic, 900 customers)

  • Days since last order
  • Orders in last 12 months
  • Average order value (₹)
  • Share of orders using a discount
  • Sentiment from reviews and tickets (−1 to +1)

Replace the generator with a CSV from your own warehouse and every chart and agent answer updates.
